Privacy Policy

Video Downloader & Saver: Luna (Media Saver) · Last updated: September 2, 2026

1. Introduction

LunaSoft ("we", "us", or "our") develops and publishes Video Downloader & Saver: Luna, also distributed as Media Saver (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App processes, how it is used, and your choices regarding your data when you use the App.

Media Saver is designed with privacy in mind. We do not require you to create an account, and we do not collect personally identifiable information.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 No Account, No Personal Information

The App does not require registration. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, or any other personally identifiable information.

2.2 Links You Submit

When you paste or enter a link to save a video, that link may be sent to our backend service (hosted on Microsoft Azure) solely to retrieve the corresponding media file. The link is used only to fulfill your request and is not associated with your identity. We do not keep a history of the links you submit on our servers. Some links are processed entirely on your device and are never sent to our servers.

2.3 Photos and Storage Access

With your permission, the App adds the videos you save to your device's photo library (on Android, to your device's Downloads or Movies folder). This is "add-only" access — the App does not read, scan, or access the existing photos or videos in your library. You can grant or revoke this permission at any time in your device Settings.

2.4 Locally Stored Data

The videos you save, your in-app library, and your preferences are stored locally on your device. This data does not leave your device and is not accessible to us. If you uninstall the App, this data is deleted.

2.5 Purchases and Subscriptions

In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Google Play (on Android) or the Apple App Store (on iOS). We do not receive or store your payment information, card numbers, or billing address. We only receive a confirmation that a purchase was made.

3. How We Use Information

The limited information processed by the App is used only to:

  • Retrieve and save the media you request.
  • Operate, maintain, and improve the App.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell, rent, or trade any user information. We do not use your data for advertising or build user profiles.

4. Third-Party Services

To operate the App, we rely on the following third-party providers:

  • Microsoft Azure (backend hosting) — privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement
  • Google Play (Android) — policies.google.com/privacy
  • Apple App Store (iOS) — apple.com/legal/privacy

These providers process data under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them.

5. Data Security

All communication between the App and our backend servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. We follow industry-standard practices to protect data in transit. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Responsibility for Content

You are responsible for ensuring you have the right to save and use any content you download with the App. Only save content you own or have permission to use, and respect the terms of service of the websites you use and all applicable copyright laws. Media Saver is a general-purpose tool and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by any third-party platform.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for users aged 13 and above. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information to us, please contact us and we will take steps to remove it.

8. Your Rights

Because we do not store personally identifiable user data on our servers, there is generally no personal data for us to access, correct, or delete. However, depending on your jurisdiction (such as the EU under GDPR or California under CCPA), you may have the following rights:

  • The right to access any data we may hold about you.
  • The right to request deletion of any data.
  • The right to object to processing.
  • The right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority.

Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain user accounts or user profiles, so there is no stored account for us to delete. Videos you save, your in-app library, and your preferences live only on your device: you can delete them at any time from within the App, or remove all of them at once by uninstalling the App. Links you submit are processed in memory to fulfil your request and are not retained on our servers. Diagnostic error logs generated when a download fails contain no personal identifiers and are automatically discarded within 30 days. If you would like to request deletion of any data you believe we hold about you, email us at info@lunasoft.com.tr and we will respond within 30 days.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email address below.
