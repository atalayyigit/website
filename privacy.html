Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

In short

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email or phone number to use the app.
  • Your saved words and progress stay on your device and in your own iCloud (iPhone, iPad) or Google backup (Android). We cannot see them.
  • We use AppsFlyer, and on iPhone and iPad also Meta, to measure which ads bring people to Luna Reader and to count subscriptions. Your device’s advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking.
  • We do not sell your data and Luna Reader shows no ads.

1. Who we are

Luna Reader is developed by Lunasoft (“we”, “us”). This policy covers the Luna Reader apps for iPhone, iPad and Android. Contact: info@lunasoft.com.tr.

We do not collect your name, email address (unless you add it to feedback), phone number, contacts, photos, precise location or health data.

Data that stays with you

Saved words, phrases, flashcard schedule, reading progress, LunAI texts, bookmarks and statistics are stored on your device. On iPhone and iPad they sync through your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), which only your Apple ID can access. On Android they may be included in your device’s Google backup. We do not receive this data.

3. Tracking and your choice

On iPhone and iPad, Luna Reader asks for permission through Apple’s App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you choose Ask App Not to Track, AppsFlyer runs in anonymous mode, the advertising identifier is not collected and Meta’s identifier collection stays off. You can change this any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Settings → Google → Ads (wording varies by device).

We use this data for ad measurement only. Luna Reader contains no ads, and we do not sell or rent personal data.

4. Subscriptions and payments

Payments are handled entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card or billing details. The app checks your subscription status with the store on your device.

5. Content from other services

Some features load content directly from third parties, who receive your IP address and standard request data when they do:

  • Music videos play through YouTube’s embedded player (Google’s privacy policy applies).
  • Song lyrics are looked up on lrclib.net.
  • News articles and images are loaded from the publishers’ public news feeds.
  • Apple Music features use your Apple Music account through Apple’s MusicKit, only if you give permission.

6. Service providers

  • AppsFlyer — ad attribution (privacy policy)
  • Meta Platforms — ad measurement, iPhone and iPad only (privacy policy)
  • Microsoft Azure — our server, storage, Azure OpenAI, Translator and Speech (privacy statement)
  • Apple — App Store, iCloud, push notifications (privacy policy)
  • Google — Google Play, YouTube (privacy policy)

Some of these providers process data outside your country, including in the United States and the European Union.

7. How long we keep data

  • Crash reports and feedback: only as long as needed to fix the problem or answer you. You can ask us to delete your feedback at any time.
  • Push tokens: until the token stops working or is replaced.
  • AI and translation requests: not stored with any identifier. To save cost, we may keep a shared table of word translations (for example “книга → book”), which contains no user information.
  • Our server uses your IP address only briefly in memory to limit abuse; it is not saved.
  • AppsFlyer and Meta keep data according to their own policies.

8. Your rights and deleting your data

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or Turkey’s KVKK), you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, or object to its use. Email info@lunasoft.com.tr. Because there are no accounts, tell us what to look for (for example the email you used in feedback).

  • Delete the app to remove data stored on your device.
  • iPhone/iPad: Settings → your name → iCloud → manage storage → Luna Reader to remove the iCloud copy.
  • Turn off tracking as described in section 3.

9. Children

Luna Reader is a general-audience reading app. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

All connections between the app and our server use HTTPS. Server keys are not stored in the app.

11. Changes

We will update this page when the app’s data practices change and show the new date at the top.

3. Takip ve senin tercihin

iPhone ve iPad’de Luna Reader, Apple’ın Uygulama Takip Şeffaflığı penceresiyle izin ister. Uygulamadan Takip Etmemesini İste seçeneğini seçersen AppsFlyer anonim modda çalışır, reklam kimliği toplanmaz ve Meta’nın kimlik toplaması kapalı kalır. Bunu istediğin zaman Ayarlar → Gizlilik ve Güvenlik → Takip bölümünden değiştirebilirsin.

Android’de Reklam Kimliğini Ayarlar → Google → Reklamlar bölümünden sıfırlayabilir ya da silebilirsin (adlar cihaza göre değişebilir).

Bu verileri yalnız reklam ölçümü için kullanıyoruz. Luna Reader’da reklam yoktur; kişisel verileri satmıyor ya da kiralamıyoruz.

4. Abonelik ve ödemeler

Ödemeleri tamamen Apple (App Store) ya da Google (Google Play) yürütür. Kart ve fatura bilgilerini hiçbir zaman görmeyiz. Uygulama abonelik durumunu cihazında mağazayla kontrol eder.

5. Başka servislerden gelen içerik

Bazı özellikler içeriği doğrudan üçüncü taraflardan yükler; bu sırada IP adresin ve standart istek bilgileri onlara ulaşır:

  • Müzik klipleri YouTube’un gömülü oynatıcısıyla oynar (Google’ın gizlilik politikası geçerlidir).
  • Şarkı sözleri lrclib.net’te aranır.
  • Haberler ve görselleri yayıncıların herkese açık haber akışlarından yüklenir.
  • Apple Music özellikleri, yalnız izin verirsen Apple’ın MusicKit’i üzerinden Apple Music hesabını kullanır.

8. Hakların ve verilerini silme

KVKK ve GDPR gibi düzenlemeler kapsamında kişisel verilerine erişmeyi, düzeltilmesini ya da silinmesini isteyebilir, işlenmesine itiraz edebilirsin. info@lunasoft.com.tr adresine yaz. Hesap olmadığı için neyi aramamız gerektiğini belirt (ör. geri bildirimde kullandığın e-posta).

  • Cihazındaki verileri silmek için uygulamayı sil.
  • iPhone/iPad: iCloud kopyasını silmek için Ayarlar → adın → iCloud → saklama alanını yönet → Luna Reader.
  • Takibi 3. bölümde anlatıldığı gibi kapat.
